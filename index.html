Questions store owners ask me

How long does a fix take?

It depends on the problem, and I'll always tell you upfront. A single bug fix is usually a matter of days once I've reproduced the issue; a full speed optimisation or a redesign of your key pages takes longer, and you'll get a written timeline with your quote before anything starts. If something turns out bigger than expected, I tell you before doing extra work — never after.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted as one fixed price after a free mini-audit of your store — no hourly billing and no open-ended invoices. You approve the written scope and price first, and that's what you pay on delivery. If I can't fix your problem, you don't pay for the attempt.

What if I don't like the result?

You see everything on a staging copy before it goes live, so there are no surprises at the end. If the result isn't what we agreed in the written scope, I revise it until it matches — that's the deal. Your live store is never touched until you've approved the work.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify and WordPress with WooCommerce are my main platforms — that's where most of my speed and bug-fix work happens. My background also covers Laravel and custom PHP, so if your store has custom code or integrations, I can usually work with those too. Not sure if your setup fits? Email me the URL and I'll tell you honestly.

Speed Optimization

Most "slow store" problems are a handful of fixable causes — not a reason to rebuild everything. I measure first, then fix what the measurements actually show.

Fixed quote upfront
  • Full speed audit — mobile and desktop, home, collection and product pages
  • Image optimisation — right sizes, modern formats, lazy loading where it helps
  • App & plugin cleanup — find the apps quietly loading scripts on every page
  • Theme code diet — remove leftover code from old themes, apps and experiments
  • Core Web Vitals pass — layout shifts, blocking scripts and font loading fixed
  • Before/after report — the same tests re-run so you can see the change yourself

Who it's for

Store owners who hear "your site is slow" from customers, see visitors leave on mobile, or are about to spend on ads and don't want to pour traffic into a slow shop.

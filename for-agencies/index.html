Focused redesigns of the pages that sell — home, product, cart — in your client's branding, previewed on staging for your approval before they go live.

Invisible by system, not just by promise

Your biggest risk with a white-label partner isn't quality — it's your client finding out. So invisibility is set up as a system at kickoff, not left as a verbal promise:

  • Commits land in your repo, under your agency's identity
  • Work happens on your staging domain, never mine
  • Deployments run through your accounts
  • No name of mine in code, metadata or screenshots
  • Your client communicates only with you — I never write to them
  • Happy to sign your NDA before I see anything

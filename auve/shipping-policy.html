Shipping & Delivery Policy

Every AUVÉ order is packed by hand at our studio in Surat, Gujarat, and shipped through trusted courier partners. This policy explains how and when your order reaches you, what it costs, and what happens if something goes wrong on the way.

Delivery locations

We deliver to most PIN codes across India through our courier partners, including metro cities, towns and most rural areas. You can check delivery to your PIN code at checkout before you pay.

For orders outside India, email contact@auve.in with your address and the pairs you would like. We will share shipping options, charges and timelines before you pay.

Order processing

Orders are processed once payment is confirmed, or once a cash-on-delivery order is confirmed by email.

Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next working day.

Dispatch time

Orders are dispatched within 48 hours (2 working days) of confirmation, Monday to Saturday.

During sales, festive seasons and peak wedding periods, dispatch may take 1 to 2 extra working days. We will tell you by email if this applies to your order.

Delivery time

The estimated delivery time for your order is shown at checkout, based on your PIN code, and is confirmed in your order confirmation email.

Delivery times are estimates from our courier partners and may vary for remote areas. During festive seasons and sale periods with high order volumes, processing or delivery timelines may vary slightly.

Shipping charges

Standard shipping is free on all orders across India.

All shipping charges are shown at checkout before you pay. There are no hidden charges at delivery for prepaid orders.

Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available on most PIN codes at no extra charge.

You can pay the courier in cash or by UPI at delivery. Please keep the exact amount ready where possible.

We may confirm cash-on-delivery orders by email before dispatch, and may decline cash on delivery for some PIN codes or repeated refused deliveries.

Packaging

Every pair is shipped in its AUVÉ box inside a protective outer carton. Please keep the box: it is required for any exchange.

Order tracking

As soon as your order is dispatched, we email you the courier name and a tracking link. You can also check your order status on the Track Order page using your order number.

Address changes

Please check your address and PIN code carefully at checkout. To change the address, email contact@auve.in before your order is dispatched. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed.

Delivery attempts

The courier will make up to 3 delivery attempts. If delivery fails because of an incorrect address, an unreachable phone number or no one being available, the order is returned to us.

Returned prepaid orders can be re-shipped once the shipping charge is paid again. Cash-on-delivery orders refused at delivery may make you ineligible for cash on delivery on future orders.

Delays

Deliveries may occasionally be delayed by weather, strikes, festivals, regional restrictions or other events outside our control. We will keep you informed by email and help you track your parcel.

Damaged, tampered or missing parcels

If the outer packaging looks damaged or tampered with, please refuse the parcel or record an unboxing video.

Email us within 48 hours of delivery with photos or the video and your order number. We will arrange a replacement at no cost once the courier confirms the issue.

If tracking shows your order as delivered but you have not received it, email us within 48 hours and we will raise a claim with the courier.

Contact

For any shipping question, email contact@auve.in with your order number. We reply within one working day.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information [Registered Business Name] (AUVÉ) collects when you use this website, how we use it and how we protect it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, mobile number, email address and delivery address when you place an order, subscribe to updates or contact us.

Order information: products, sizes, amounts, payment method and order status.

Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive only the payment status and a transaction reference, never your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank login details.

Technical information: device and browser type, pages visited and similar data, used to keep the site secure and working well.

How we use it

To process, deliver and support your orders, including exchanges and cancellations.

To send order and delivery updates by email.

To answer your questions and provide customer support.

To send offers and new arrivals by email, only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or by writing to us.

To prevent fraud and meet our legal and tax obligations.

Who we share it with

Courier partners, to deliver your order.

Razorpay, our payment partner, to process payments and any refunds.

Government or law-enforcement authorities, where required by law.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

This site stores your bag, recent orders and sign-in in your browser so they are there when you return. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable security practices to protect your information. We keep it only as long as needed to fulfil orders, handle exchanges and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Your rights

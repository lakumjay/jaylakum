Orders received

Every order placed in the shop appears here, newest first. "Cash on delivery" means the customer pays when the parcel arrives. "Paid online" means the money is already received. "TEST" means the shop was in test mode and no money was taken.

Online payments: Razorpay keys

Razorpay is the company that takes card and UPI payments for your shop. When your keys are saved here, online payment starts working on its own. Until then, the shop stays in test mode and no real money is taken.

Where to find your keys: log in at dashboard.razorpay.com. Open Settings, then API Keys, then press Generate Key. Copy the Key ID and the Key Secret. The Key Secret is shown only once, so copy it right away and paste it here.

Delivery: Shiprocket login

Shiprocket books the courier when an order comes in. When your login is saved here, shipments are created on their own. Until then, orders still arrive here, you just book the courier yourself.

What to type: the same email and password you use to log in at app.shiprocket.in. The pickup location name must match a pickup address name in your Shiprocket account. The usual name is Primary.

Change your password

Pick a password that is easy for you to remember and hard for others to guess. After you change it, the old password stops working at once.

Your recovery code

A recovery code is a secret word or short phrase that only you know, like a pet name plus a number. If you ever forget your password, the login page asks for this code and lets you choose a new password right away. No email is needed.

Checking...

Write your code down and keep it somewhere safe, like a notebook at home. Anyone who knows this code can get into your shop manager, so do not share it.

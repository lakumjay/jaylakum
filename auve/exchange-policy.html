Exchange, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Every AUVÉ pair is made by hand in small batches, so we offer exchanges instead of refunds on delivered orders. Here is exactly how exchanges, cancellations and refunds work.

Exchanges

You can exchange a pair for a different size, or for another design of equal value, within 5 days of delivery.

To request an exchange, email contact@auve.in with your order number and the size or design you would like.

We arrange the pickup and dispatch the replacement once the original pair reaches us and passes inspection, usually within 3 to 5 working days. If your choice is unavailable, you receive store credit of the same value, valid for 12 months.

Conditions for exchange

The pair must be unused, unworn, clean and undamaged.

It must be returned in the original box with all packaging and tags.

Not eligible for exchange

Pairs that have been used or worn.

Pairs damaged after delivery.

Pairs without the original box or packaging.

Requests made more than 5 days after delivery.

Wrong or defective pair

If you receive the wrong product or a pair with a manufacturing defect, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the shoes and the box. We will replace it at no cost to you, including pickup.

Refunds

We do not offer refunds on delivered orders, except where a wrong or defective pair cannot be replaced.

Refunds are issued only when an order is cancelled before dispatch, when we cannot fulfil an order, or when a wrong or defective pair cannot be replaced.

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID you provide.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order free of charge any time before it is dispatched. Email your order number to contact@auve.in. Prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full.

Orders cannot be cancelled once dispatched; you can request an exchange after delivery instead.

Charges

Pickup for eligible exchanges is free. Shipping and cash on delivery are free, so there are no charges to refund.

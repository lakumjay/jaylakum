The Challenge: Strong Offline Reputation, Invisible on Google

The client operates a reputable local service business in Gothenburg, Sweden. Despite having satisfied customers and years of word-of-mouth recommendations, their website failed to capture organic search demand. When potential clients in Gothenburg searched for localized service solutions on mobile devices, competitors with weaker service offerings dominated the top search results.

Our initial technical crawl using Screaming Frog and Google Search Console revealed three foundational bottlenecks:

  • Flat Architecture: Multiple distinct service offerings were bundled onto a single page, diluting topical relevance.
  • Missing Semantic Schema: Zero structured data existed to establish entity relationships with Gothenburg geographic coordinates.
  • Inconsistent Off-Page Signals: Business citations across local directories contained mismatched phone formats and outdated addresses.

The Process: 5-Step Technical & Local Search Framework

1. Deep Technical Crawl & Indexation Cleanup

We resolved duplicate URL variants, enforced self-referencing canonical tags, and eliminated 301 redirect chains. We verified mobile viewport responsiveness across standard iOS Safari and Android Chrome resolutions.

2. Dedicated Local Service URL Hierarchy

Rather than relying on generic site-wide copy, we restructured the website into dedicated landing pages targeting explicit localized intent queries in Gothenburg and surrounding municipalities.

3. Semantic On-Page Optimization & Heading Architecture

Every page received unique, intent-matching title tags under 60 characters, benefit-driven meta descriptions under 940 pixels, and a clean single H1 heading hierarchy without skips.

4. LocalBusiness JSON-LD Knowledge Graph Integration

We injected comprehensive LocalBusiness schema connecting physical coordinates, opening hours, official service categories, and verified social entity links (sameAs) directly into Google's Knowledge Graph.

5. 100% Consistent Citation & NAP Cleanup

We audited Swedish business directories and local platforms, systematically correcting Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) details. Zero link farms or private blog networks were used — every signal was strictly white-hat.

The Outcome: 300+ Ranked Queries and Sustained Inbound Calls

Within months of implementing the technical fixes and localized hierarchy, Google rewarded the domain with rapid keyword expansion:

  • Over 300 organic search terms indexed and tracked across Google Sweden.
  • Top-3 and Page-1 rankings for core commercial intent keywords in the Gothenburg metro area.
  • Direct inbound leads: Increased organic phone calls and quote requests without ongoing paid advertising spend.
